# joaoferrari.com — short PRD

Status: discovery draft, awaiting João's updates. October 28, 2026.

## Purpose

Create a personal home that explains who João Ferrari is, shows what he is building and learning, and helps relevant people start a conversation. The website should grow alongside his work and make the process of building the site a public project in its own right.

## Proposed positioning

Student, builder, and curious thinker working across entrepreneurship, AI, and education. The connecting thread is helping people turn ideas and ambition into useful action.

Draft introduction: “I'm João Ferrari. I build tools and bring people together around ideas worth pursuing.” Follow with one concrete sentence about current work, once confirmed. Keep the voice direct, personal, and grounded in examples.

## Audience and outcome

Proposed primary audience: potential collaborators and mentors in startups, education, and research. Secondary: people exploring João's background and work. Confirm priority before choosing the final headline.

Within 30 seconds, a visitor should understand who João is, see one credible example of his work, and know how to connect. Primary action: get in touch. Secondary: explore a project.

## First version

- **Introduction:** name, short bio, supplied portrait if confirmed, current focus, and clear links to work/contact.
- **Selected work:** three substantial stories, provisionally Vocanize, AI/consumer-decision research, and Nido Hack/IE&T. Each explains the problem, personal role, stage, work produced, evidence, and next question. Research must be labeled according to its actual stage; institutional involvement needs confirmation.
- **About:** a short personal story connecting Brazilian roots, experiences in Chile/Panama, building, curiosity, and football. Confirm current biographical details.
- **Now:** a dated paragraph describing current priorities, easy to update.
- **Contact:** confirmed public email and professional links. Add a résumé download only after updating and reviewing it for public use.
- **This website:** a small project entry linking the public repository, PRD, decisions, and eventual screenshots.

Start with one coherent homepage and individual project pages where enough content exists. Add writing only when there is an actual piece to publish.

## Visual direction — proposed

Warm editorial: off-white background, charcoal text, deep green accents, generous whitespace, large expressive headings, and readable body text. Use the portrait to make the introduction human and project screenshots to make the work tangible. Prefer a few strong sections to a dashboard of cards. Motion should be subtle and respect reduced-motion settings. English first, pending confirmation.

See [visual brief](visual-direction.md) for references and layout.

## Requirements and launch criteria

- Responsive at phone, tablet, and desktop widths; no horizontal overflow.
- Semantic headings, keyboard navigation, visible focus, descriptive links, alt text, and WCAG AA color contrast.
- Real project links, accurate roles/dates, and evidence for every published metric. No invented testimonials, traction, partnerships, or publication claims.
- Compressed images, stable image dimensions, useful page titles/descriptions, social preview, and sitemap.
- HTTPS on joaoferrari.com, one canonical domain, and working contact links, verified after hosting is selected.
- Public repository includes setup instructions, this PRD, content guidance, decisions, and website source when implementation begins. Keep updating a project possible through simple content edits.
- Check the complete experience on mobile and desktop before launch. No placeholder copy or empty pages in the published version.

## Scope and sequence

1. Confirm audience, current facts, desired action, visual preference, and public contact details.
2. Gather evidence and write three concise project stories; choose references and sketch the homepage.
3. Build a responsive preview; review content, appearance, accessibility, and links.
4. Publish the repository and site, connect the domain, then document the finished project.

Success: visitors can describe João's current work and reach him easily; João can add a project without redesigning the site. Track useful conversations and projects kept current. Analytics are optional for version one.

Defer: CMS, login, chatbot, elaborate animation, newsletter infrastructure, and multilingual versions. The main tradeoff is breadth versus depth: launch with three credible stories before expanding the collection.

## Open decisions

Who is the first audience? What is current versus historical? Which three projects have shareable evidence? Which design references feel right? What contact information and portrait should be public?
